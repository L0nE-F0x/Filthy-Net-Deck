Why this page exists

Filthy Net Deck reads Magic: The Gathering Arena's own log file to record your matches. That format is unofficial — Wizards can change it in any client update, without notice, and when they do, match tracking can break for everybody at once through no fault of your install.

This page is where that gets said out loud. If tracking has broken, it will say so here, and reinstalling or fiddling with your settings will not help — a new build is the fix, and it will arrive through the app's own updater.

If your matches are not being recorded

Two things are worth checking before assuming it is us:

  • Detailed Logs. Arena → Options → Account → Detailed Logs (Plugin Support) must be on. The app will tell you if it is off, and Arena needs a restart after enabling it.
  • You are on the current build. Settings → Updates → Check for updates.

If both are fine and this page says everything is normal, that is worth reporting — open an issue and attach the diagnostic from Settings → Tracker health. It contains no match data and no account details.

What this page does not cover

The daily meta feed is built by a pipeline that aborts rather than publishing anything it could not verify, so if a source goes down the app keeps showing the last real data with an "offline" marker rather than going blank or inventing lists. That is working as intended, not an outage.
