Features

Everything you need to stay filthy-current

One native app for the two constructed formats that actually have a public paper metagame. Daily boards, a mid-match overlay or companion window, and a winrate tracker that stays on this PC unless you opt in.

Presence badge

Always know it's running.

The match overlay only shows up once a game starts — on the home screen or in the deck builder you were left guessing. Now a small badge sits bottom-left for as long as Arena is open: the mark, a live dot, and a ⚙ holding the settings actually worth changing between games — overlay on/off, post-match summary, opacity, fullscreen alerts. It dims while you're in a match so the HUD stays the main event, and wakes the moment you hover it. Click the mark to jump straight back to the app.

Coverage

Two formats. Done properly.

No Alchemy. No rebalanced digital-only cards. Just the two constructed formats where the paper metagame is real, public, and trackable — covered with verified lists only.

The data promise: every list in the app is a real, current build from the live metagame. Every card name is checked against Scryfall before it ships. If a deck can’t be verified, it doesn’t appear — the app never shows placeholder, AI-guessed, or “seeded” decks.
